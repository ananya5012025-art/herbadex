Pharmacognosy

Pharmacognosy is the study of medicines and useful substances obtained from natural sources, especially plants.

Phytochemistry

Phytochemistry focuses on the chemical constituents found in plants, including compounds such as alkaloids, flavonoids, terpenoids and phenolic compounds.
